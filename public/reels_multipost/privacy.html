Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. What Reels Multipost is

Reels Multipost is a personal desktop application operated by Turn 1 Visuals, used only by its owner to upload self-created videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

2. Data collected

The application does not collect, store, or process any data from third parties. It has no users other than its operator, no analytics, no tracking, and no server component.

3. Platform authorization

When connecting a social media account (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X), the application receives OAuth access tokens for the operator's own accounts. These tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never transmitted anywhere other than to the respective platform's official API.

4. Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. The only data sent to the connected platforms is the video content and metadata the operator chooses to publish.

5. Data retention and deletion

Access tokens can be deleted at any time by the operator by removing them locally or revoking the application's access in the platform's account settings.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via turn1visuals.com.
